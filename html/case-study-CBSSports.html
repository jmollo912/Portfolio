CBS SPORTS | WSM

Contract

Year 2025
Duration 10 weeks

Overview

I was selected to be on a team with one HUGE goal...

To completely overhaul the World’s Strongest Man Competition’s Live Broadcast GFX package for CBS SPORTS. I lead the research phase for our final deliverables as well as helped lead the overall art direction of the final graphics package. While also presenting our final deliverables to CBS Sports directly.

Research

Understanding the broadcast landscape.

I led a group of 20 designers in the research phase for this project. We first moodboarded multiple concepts and took inspiration from multiple design sources.

I presented our first 8 concepts to CBS Sports stakeholders along with 3 of my team members. We received extremely positive feedback and were able to combine the best features from each concept into our final design moving forward.
