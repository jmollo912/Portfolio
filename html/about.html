About me

Growing up in Philadelphia, sports were a way of life. That fast paced environment shaped how I approach design. At its core, sports are about adapting and solving problems in real time, a mindset I bring to every project.

I originally attended SCAD to pursue graphic design, but one UX course was all it took for me to fall in love with building digital and physical products.

Today, as a product designer, I have incorporated AI into my design workflow to help me build better products faster.
